What happened:
We caught up to a dark ERJ-145 on a towbarless tug while taxiing a B757 to the hangar. We stopped before reaching it.

What almost happened:
A collision with the aircraft in tow.

Phase of flight:
Taxi

Primary factor:
Equipment

Contributing factors:
- No external lighting
- Tug obscured by the aircraft

Recoverable:
Yes
We stopped before reaching it.

Lesson:
Towed aircraft should be visible to other traffic.
